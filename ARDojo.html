01 / SYSTEM VIEW

One field loop. Four layers.

SPARROW connects solar-powered sensing, edge inference, remote transfer and human review. This workshop zooms into the neural-network layer without losing sight of the real deployment.

SENSE

Camera + acoustic capture

Time-stamped images and audio windows become the raw evidence. Sampling rate, gain, placement and power budget shape everything downstream.

INFER

ONNX at the edge

A portable graph packages learned weights and operations for consistent inference across Python, servers and constrained devices.

REVIEW

Human feedback

People confirm events, reject false positives and label uncertain species. Store provenance instead of silently overwriting predictions.

ADAPT

Field-specific learning

Curated corrections become training data for local habitats, microphones, cameras and target taxa.

03 / MODEL MAP

Know what each net does.

Detection answers “where and what broad class?” Classification answers “which species?” Acoustic inference answers “what vocalized, and when?” ONNX is the exchange format and runtime bridge—not a model architecture.

MegaDetectorImage → boxes + animal/person/vehicle scoresObject detector; screens camera-trap media before species review.Inspect preprocessing, box coordinates, confidence and NMS behavior.

✓
