The practice

Nature is not the backdrop. It is the teacher.

Wilderness Dojo brings observation, restoration and experimental making into one open-air practice. We build tools to hear other species, revive living habitats and learn directly from wild systems.
01 / LISTEN

Bioacoustics

Hear patterns beyond the human voice through patient field recording and machine listening.

02 / RESTORE

Living systems

Restore attention, habitat and reciprocal relationships with the more-than-human world.

03 / MAKE

Field technology

Build open tools for observation—from automatic recording units to offline plant intelligence.
