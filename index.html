The practice

Nature is not the backdrop. It is the teacher.

Wilderness Dojo brings observation, restoration and experimental making into one open-air practice. We build tools to hear other species, revive living habitats and learn resilience directly from the wild.
01 / LISTEN

Bioacoustics

Autonomous recording units, interspecies phonetics and AI-assisted listening for richer ecological awareness.

02 / RESTORE

Rewilding

Sacred groves, wild edibles and community habitats that reconnect human wellbeing with ecological repair.

03 / MAKE

Fieldcraft

Solar light, sensor meshes and nature-inspired structures—practical experiments built for life outside.
